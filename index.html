    The best explorer for each planet

    Many spacecraft visited the same planets. These are the ones that taught us the most.

    PlanetSpacecraftWhy it wins
    MercuryMESSENGEROrbited for 4 years, mapped the whole planet and found ice in dark craters at the poles.
    VenusMagellanUsed radar to see through the clouds and mapped about 98% of the surface.
    EarthLandsatHas photographed Earth's land for over 50 years, so we can see how it changes.
    MarsCuriosityFound that Mars once had a lake that could have supported tiny life.
    JupiterGalileoOrbited for 8 years, dropped a probe into Jupiter and found hints of an ocean on Europa.
    SaturnCassiniStudied Saturn for 13 years, found geysers on Enceladus and landed a probe on Titan.
    UranusVoyager 2The only spacecraft that has ever visited.
    NeptuneVoyager 2The only spacecraft that has ever visited.

    These spacecraft have launched but haven't finished their trip yet.

    BepiColombo

    Two orbiters riding together to Mercury, due to arrive in late 2026 to study its surface and magnetic field.

    Launched 2023ESA

    JUICE

    Heading to Jupiter, arriving in 2031, to study the icy moons Ganymede, Callisto and Europa.

    Launched 2024NASA

    Europa Clipper

    Arriving at Jupiter in 2030 to find out if the ocean under Europa's ice could support life.
